## 2. Content Quality

### Thin pages (<200 words of content)

| Section | Thin | Total | % Thin |
|---------|------|-------|--------|

| Races | 501 | 1,163 | 43% |

| Constructors | 44 | 205 | 21% |
| Circuits | 0 | 78 | 0% |

**537 driver pages** are under 200 words. These are mostly obscure historical drivers with 1–5 starts, and their About sections are the bulk of the non-tabular content.

### Mad-libs driver narratives (scaled content risk)

- **605 of 818** (74%) driver About sections open with the same frame: "[Name], a/an [nationality] driver born in [year], ..."
- Another 130 use "is/was a/an [nationality]..."
- Only 82 have genuinely unique openings

While the text is not copy-pasted (each bio has different details), the structural uniformity at scale is a scaled-content signal. This was flagged in the July audit as the **main scaled-content risk**.

### Meta descriptions promising absent content

- **723 driver meta descriptions** mention "race results" — no driver page has individual race results (only season aggregates)
- **615 driver meta descriptions** mention "qualifying record" — no qualifying data exists on any driver page
- Example: *"Carlo Abate, Italian driver, started 2 F1 Grands Prix... Full race results, qualifying record and season-by-season career stats."* — the page has neither race results nor qualifying data

This is a **misleading meta description** issue that could hurt CTR when users don't find what was promised.

### Meta descriptions — good

- Every page has a **unique** meta description (0 duplicates across 2,965 pages)
- Only 3 meta descriptions exceed 160 characters
- 0 pages are missing meta descriptions

### Title lengths

- 37 titles exceed 60 characters (longest: 76 chars)
- Most are records/decades pages with descriptive titles — low priority

### Structured data issues

**FAQPage schema without visible FAQ (Google manual-action risk):**
8 records pages have FAQPage structured data with Q&A pairs, but **no visible FAQ section** on the page:
- `records/most-championships/`
- `records/most-poles/`
- `records/most-fastest-laps/`
- `records/most-starts/`
- `records/most-wins/`
- `records/most-podiums/`
- `records/constructor-wins/`
- `records/constructor-championships/`

The guide pages (wet-weather, reliability, etc.) have FAQPage schema **with** visible FAQ sections — these are fine.

**EventStatus wrong on ALL 1,163 race pages:**
Every race page, including completed races from 1950 to 2026, has `"eventStatus": "EventScheduled"`. Past races should be `"EventCompleted"`.

**Calendar page fake "Offers" schema:**
The calendar page's SportsEvent schema includes ticket `"Offers"` with `"price": "0"` and `"url": "https://pitlanestats.com/calendar/"`. PitlaneStats does not sell tickets. This is misleading structured data that could trigger a Google manual action.

## 4. Internal Linking

### Good

- Race result tables link to all drivers and constructors mentioned
- Driver index pagination covers 814 of 818 indexed drivers
- H2H index page links to all 150+ individual H2H pages
- On-this-day pages link to individual driver pages
- Driver pages link to their H2H comparisons (819/818 pages)

### Issues

**H2H pages don't link to specific drivers (0/150):**
Every H2H page (e.g., `head-to-head/alonso-vs-button/`) links only to the generic `/drivers/` index, never to `/drivers/alonso/` or `/drivers/button/`. This is a major missed linking opportunity.

**Race pages have no prev/next navigation (0/1,163):**
No race page links to the previous or next race in the season. Users must go back to the season page to navigate between races.

**Race pages don't link to season pages:**
The race results show the season year but don't link back to `/seasons/YYYY/`.

**40 nationality pages are completely orphaned:**
`drivers/nationality/*` has 40 pages in the sitemap with **zero inlinks** from any other page on the site. These are indexed but undiscoverable via internal navigation.

**4 drivers missing from index pagination:**
`campbell-jones`, `lewis-evans`, `mackay-fraser`, `scott_Brown` — linked from race result pages but not from any driver index page.

**/races/ duplicates /seasons/:**
The `/races/` index links only to season pages (77 links), providing the same function as `/seasons/`. It has only 2 inlinks (homepage + seasons index). Consider merging or differentiating.

**Races index shows stale count:**
The `/races/` index says "2026 9 races" — the 2026 season page says 14 (both are behind the real 16).

## 6. Accessibility & UX

### WCAG Contrast Issues

| Combination | Ratio | WCAG AA (4.5:1) |
|------------|-------|-----------------|
| f1-red (#E10600) on f1-dark (#111118) | **3.78:1** | **FAIL** |
| f1-red (#E10600) on f1-card (#1A1A26) | **3.47:1** | **FAIL** |
| f1-text (#8E8EA0) on f1-dark (#111118) | 5.84:1 | PASS |
| f1-text (#8E8EA0) on f1-card (#1A1A26) | 5.35:1 | PASS |
| f1-white (#EEEEF0) on f1-dark (#111118) | 16.22:1 | PASS |

**F1 red fails WCAG AA for normal text.** It passes for large text (3:1 threshold), so large headings/stat numbers are acceptable, but small red links, labels, and stat text fail. This affects the entire site since red is the primary accent color used for links, stat highlights, and badges.

### Tables

All tables use `overflow-x-auto` wrappers — tables scroll horizontally on mobile rather than overflowing the page. **Good.**

### Rating abbreviations

No abbreviation issues found. Stats use clear labels (Wins, Podiums, Poles, etc.).
